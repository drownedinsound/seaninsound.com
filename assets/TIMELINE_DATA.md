## 1997–2000: The Dorset years

### 1997 — Record shop assistant, FM Music / Falcon Records
"More like Empire Records than High Fidelity"

### 1997 — Club promoter, Verdis Weymouth
Weekly live nights booking Ash, Cooper Temple Clause, jj72, Toploader. Also built the venue's website.
`live`

### 1998 — The Last Resort email fanzine launched ★
Reviewed first demos by Muse, Coldplay, Cooper Temple Clause. Some of their very first interviews.
`publication`

---

## 2000–2008: Building the ecosystem

### 2000 — Drowned in Sound launched ★
Website, community forums (still running: 2–3m pageviews/month today), club nights at the Barfly, Dublin Castle, Notting Hill Arts Club.
`publication` `live`

### 2003 — DiS Recordings launched ★
Debut releases by Kaiser Chiefs, Bat for Lashes, Emmy the Great, Martha Wainwright (80k CDs), Metric, The Stills, Brett Anderson.
`label`

### 2005 — DiS podcast launched
One of the UK's first music podcasts. Won Best Podcast, Record of the Day 2006.
`publication`

### 2006 — Observer: DiS ranked #9 Top 25 Websites
Best Online Music Publication (Record of the Day 2007). NME, BT Digital Awards nominations.
`publication`

### 2008 — The Quietus launched from DiS
Spawned and funded from a DiS ad revenue advance. Now one of the UK's most respected music publications.
`publication`

### 2008 — Love Music Hate Racism partnership
Tours and LMHR Carnival, Victoria Park. Music fandom as anti-fascist coalition building.
`political`

## 2009–2019: Peak reach and new roles

### 2009 — Sunday Times "My Generation" column
Fortnightly column on music × technology × commerce, Culture section (to 2012).
`media`

### 2010 — Spotify playlist curator
Monthly playlists for Spotify's official blog (to 2011). Best Publication, Record of the Day 2010.
`publication`

### 2012 — Artist management begins (CCCLX Music)
Ed Harcourt, The Anchoress. Ed produced Sophie Ellis-Bextor's comeback, wrote for Paloma Faith, James Bay; Burberry campaign.
`management`

### 2015 — Independent Music Monday playlists
Weekly playlist celebrating best indie label releases, supported by [PIAS] (to 2017).
`publication`

### 2015 — Soho Radio host
Regular show for nearly six years (to 2021).
`media`

### 2016 — BBC Radio 6 Music — Social Media Producer
Managed social strategy, created viral content. Also covered BBC Radio 2 (to 2021).
`media`

### 2019 — DiS goes on hiatus
Ad rates collapsed from £3–8 CPM to 25p. 3 million annual readers but the business model broke. Community forums continued.

## 2020–2024: Rebuilding and diversifying

### 2021 — Head of Digital, Studio Richter Mahr
Three album campaigns with Max Richter alongside his work with UN Human Rights (to 2022).
`media`

### 2022 — Charlotte Church — artist management
Navigating her transition to politically engaged independent artist (to early 2026).
`management`

### 2022 — The Trawl — podcast producer
Political podcast with Marina Purkiss & Jemma Forte. Subsequently joined Goalhanger (Gary Lineker) family.
`media`

### 2023 — DiS relaunched as newsletter + podcast ★
Moved from Substack to Ghost for ethical reasons. Mission: surfacing music's power for change.
`publication`

### 2024 — StopTheTories.vote — strategic comms
Tactical voting campaign with Carol Vorderman. Reached 1.75 million people.
`political`

### 2024 — Music Fans Voice survey
8,000 participants. Helped drive UK government fan-led review of live music.
`political`
