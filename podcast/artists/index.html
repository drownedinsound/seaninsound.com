Taylor Swift

  • AI & Consent Special with Addison Rae, Dua Lipa and Taylor... One of three named stories: she trademarked her own likeness, and the hosts ask whether that can slow deepfakes.
  • The Stories of 2025 - Part 1: Megagigs, Grassroots, and AI... Used as the example of mega-gig wealth, with the hosts doubting how communal that scale of fandom really is.
  • You! Me! 10 Million Spotify Streams! How Much?!?!! Why Los... Cited as the release-month rival that makes streaming payouts a zero-sum contest between artists.

  • Maggie Baird on How Music Can Save The World | Episode 1 Her mother Maggie Baird explains taking the Support + Feed food and waste programme on tour with Billie and Finneas.
  • How Music Fans Can Save The Planet - Tori Tsui on Billie... Tori Tsui describes working with Billie on her Overheated climate events and what smaller artists can borrow.
  • What Great Music Journalists Hear That Algorithms Miss: DiS... Journalist Ilana Kaplan tells the story of spotting her at an early New York show.

Dua Lipa

  • AI & Consent Special with Addison Rae, Dua Lipa and Taylor... Her lawsuit against Samsung over her photo on a TV box is the closing consent story, and the hosts note it was not AI-generated.

Addison Rae

  • Albums of the Year: Emma Wilkes & Sean Adams Pick Their... Her self-titled album is on the hosts' 2025 albums list.

  • Enter Shikari exclusive: Rou Reynolds on why the band goes... Cited as one of the acts that followed Enter Shikari's pound-per-ticket grassroots venue scheme.
