Enter Shikari: protecting nature from billionaires

With Sean Adams and Helena Wadia. 29 October 2026. 58 minutes.

Play on AppleSpotifyWatch on YouTube

What happens when a band that has spent 25 years shouting about the system decides to organise inside it?

Rou Reynolds joins us a few days before the tour starts, and we start where we always do, with a record (the first one he ever bought, a decision he is still not sure about). Placeholder copy to show the drop cap and the measure of the reading column.
