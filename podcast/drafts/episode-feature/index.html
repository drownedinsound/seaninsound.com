Why Does Electronic Music Sound Like Shoegaze? Art School Girlfriend on Lean In, positive nihilism, and making music for fun

Polly Mackey of Art School Girlfriend nearly walked away from releasing music, then built a studio and made Lean In. She and Sean talk about shoegaze, electronic music and what 'democratised' music costs.

Lean In is her third album, out on Fiction Records.

Apple PodcastsEvery podcast app (pod.link)Podbean
Polly Mackey

What came up

  1. Lean In is her third album, out on Fiction Records.
  2. She completed a Master's thesis on how electronic music can feel human.
  3. Ableton water-simulation plugins shaped the sound.
  4. They ask whether the album format is in crisis and who benefits when music is democratised.
