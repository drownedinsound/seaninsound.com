Our mission

We believe that music is upstream from politics.

In each episode we meet an expert or someone with first-hand experience of systemic injustice, and ask about the solutions to build a better (music) world.

Episodes

What we keep coming back to

Live music and ticketing

Resellers stand to make an estimated £24 million from fans in summer 2026. So why has nothing changed?

Streaming and fair pay

Artists are taking their music down. Here is what we learned, and what to do next.

AI and tech

Contracts signed in 2020 could not have covered AI uses artists did not yet understand. Who consented?

Grassroots and scenes

What if promoters, venues and fans pledged to put money back into the local scene?

Safety and inclusion

Why the system still protects abusers.

Climate

82% of music fans want to stop climate breakdown, but only 3% know what to do.

Class and access

Who gets to make music, and who gets to hear it live? From a tour bus costing nearly £50,000 to free tickets for carers and NHS workers.

Mental health & wellbeing

From panic attacks on stage to the impacts of our online world.

Journalism and media

Where is music journalism going, and who pays for it? We ask TikTokkers, rock writers, war correspondents, and tech reporters.

Meet the hosts

Sean Adams

Founder of Drowned in Sound, host of the DiS Podcast

Sean Adams founded Drowned in Sound in 1998 as a newsletter and launched it as a website in 2000, and now hosts the DiS Podcast with Helena Wadia.

  • BBC Radio 6 Music
  • The Sunday Times
  • Studio Richter Mahr
  • Association of Music Editors
  • Charlotte Church (manager)
  • The Trawl (producer)

Instagram · Bluesky · LinkedIn · X

Helena Wadia

Multimedia journalist, presenter and co-creator of Media Storm, co-host of the DiS Podcast

Helena Wadia is a multimedia journalist and award-winning presenter who works across print, video and audio, and co-created the Media Storm podcast with Mathilda Mallinson.

  • Media Storm: British Podcast Awards gold, ARIAs silver (2022)
  • NME In Conversation
  • Evening Standard
  • London Live
  • Channel 5 News
  • BBC Asian Network

Instagram · X · Media Storm · The Line of Best Fit

Shain Shapiro

Host of How Music Can Save The World

Shain Shapiro, PhD, is an international music policy expert and the founder of Sound Diplomacy.
