Meet the hosts

SA

Sean Adams

Founder of Drowned in Sound, host of the DiS Podcast

Sean Adams founded Drowned in Sound in 1998 as a newsletter and launched it as a website in 2000, and now hosts the DiS Podcast with Helena Wadia. He is a former digital producer at BBC Radio 6 Music and former Sunday Times columnist, and he helped launch The Quietus.

Has worked with:

  • BBC Radio 6 Music
  • The Sunday Times
  • Max Richter and Yulia Mahr's studio
  • Association of Music Editors
  • The Sounding
HW

Helena Wadia

Multimedia journalist, presenter and co-creator of Media Storm, co-host of the DiS Podcast

Helena Wadia is a multimedia journalist and presenter who works across print, video and audio, and co-created the Media Storm podcast with Mathilda Mallinson. She has presented NME's In Conversation series, anchored London Live's News at Six and was a senior video journalist at the Evening Standard.

Has worked with:

Why this exists

Start with the music, follow it somewhere political

Drowned in Sound started as a website 25 years ago, launched its first podcast in 2005, and carries on as a newsletter and a podcast network. The podcast is where artists and campaigners sit down to talk about what happens when music and power collide, and what we might build instead.

It is for music fans who care where their money goes and who it reaches, artists who want to know what is possible, and campaigners who want a room that listens.
