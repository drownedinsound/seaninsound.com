What is this?

Start with the music, follow it somewhere political

Drowned in Sound started as a website 25 years ago, launched its first podcast in 2005, and carries on as a newsletter and a podcast network. The podcast is where artists and campaigners sit down to talk about what happens when music and power collide, and what we might build instead.

Music is upstream from politics: the records people love lead, sooner or later, to questions about who gets paid, who gets in and who is listening. These conversations follow that thread, and ask how fans, artists and workers can organise for something fairer.

Who it is for

Music fans who care where their money goes and who it reaches, and who are looking for fairer ways to stream, buy tickets and back artists. Artists who want to know what is possible. Campaigners who want a room that listens.

One question for every guest

How would you spend £500 million to fix the music industry?

We ask every guest the same closing question. Other questions change from conversation to conversation, but this one never does.
