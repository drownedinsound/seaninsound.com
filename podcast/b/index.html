What is this?

Start with the music, follow it somewhere political

Drowned in Sound started as a website 25 years ago and carries on as a newsletter and a podcast network. The podcast is where artists and campaigners sit down to talk about what happens when music and power collide, and what we might build instead.

Music is upstream from politics: the records people love lead, sooner or later, to questions about who gets paid, who gets in and who is listening. These conversations follow that thread.

Who it is for

People who love music and sense something is off in how it is made, paid for and heard. Artists who want to know what is possible. Campaigners who want a room that listens.
