Maggie Baird on How Music Can Save The World | Episode 1

29 October 2026 · 60 min · with Shain Shapiro

Shain meets Maggie Baird, mother of Billie Eilish and Finneas, who founded Support + Feed during COVID and has taken it on tour with them. Sean joins afterwards to talk about access and The Ticket Bank.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Support + Feed began as a way to feed people during COVID, plant-based, and later toured with Billie, Finneas and Hayley Williams.
  2. It has changed how stadiums and arenas handle waste, food and water.
  3. Musicians have a direct line to fans that actors and directors lack.
  4. Maggie quotes Joan Baez: action is the antidote to despair.
  5. The Ticket Bank has given out more than a million tickets.
