Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 3 · Episode 28

How to Empower Music’s Next Generation - DiSpatch from the Youth Music Awards

24 October 2025 · 43 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Emma Wilkes reports from Youth Music's awards at the Troxy, with 41% of grassroots community music spaces at risk of closure hanging over the night. She talks to industry leaders, organisers and rising artists about equal access to music and what fans can do.

Key takeaways

  1. 41% of grassroots community music spaces are at risk of closure because of financial pressure.
  2. Youth Music is the UK's largest music charity, funding hundreds of organisations and supporting over 100,000 young people a year.
  3. Sony's Charlotte Edgeworth, Lyrix Organix's Dan Tsu, Youth Music CEO Matt Griffiths and Faithless's Sister Bliss each make the case for opportunity at the start of a young person's creative life.
  4. Rescue the Roots is Youth Music's call to action, with resources for fans who want to help.
