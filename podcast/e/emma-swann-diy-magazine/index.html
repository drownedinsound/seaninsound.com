Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 2 · Episode 5

Lessons from two decades of crafting DIY Magazine

31 October 2023 · 68 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

DIY Magazine co-founder Emma Swann joins Sean to compare notes on running a music website and moving into print. We talk human curation versus algorithms and whether magazines could make a comeback.

Key takeaways

  1. Emma traces DIY's journey from a website to its current print and online format.
  2. She argues print offers a pause and a moment of engagement you will not find online.
  3. Human curation is weighed against algorithms.
  4. Emma also works in photography, writing, podcasting and web design.
  5. They get distracted by the new Bully album, in a good way.
