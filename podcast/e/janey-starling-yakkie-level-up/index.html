How Do You Turn Rage Into A Strategy? | Janey Starling from Yakkie

11 November 2026 · 65 min

Helena and Sean ask Janey Starling of Yakkie and Level Up how anger becomes strategy and how change actually gets made. It covers the Dignity for Dead Women campaign, Bands Boycott Barclays and how the media reports suicide.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Janey bottles anger, gives it a strategy and has the staying power to see it through.
  2. Level Up's Dignity for Dead Women campaign took media guidelines on reporting fatal domestic abuse into newsrooms.
  3. Culture changes before the law does.
  4. She helped organise the Bands Boycott Barclays campaign.
  5. The episode contains a content note covering sexual violence, domestic homicide and suicide.
