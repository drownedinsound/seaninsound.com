Can You Hear Earth Singing? Meet Musicians Using Sound to Protect the Planet

12 April 2025 · 65 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

A live panel from Tallinn Music Week with artists from the EarthSonic project, using field recordings, hydrophones and plant biofeedback to make the planet audible. It is urgent but hopeful, about sound as a way into caring.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Hydrophones let us hear melting glaciers.
  2. Natural Symphony collaborates with plants and is working on reforesting the Amazon.
  3. Martyn Ware talks about sonifying endangered species and synthetic forests.
  4. Emotional shifts through sound can drive cultural change.
  5. In Place of War has a summer 2025 project with Bicep in Greenland.
