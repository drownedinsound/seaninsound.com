Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 2 · Episode 9

If $600 billion a year is being spent, why are so many publications closing?

14 November 2023 · 68 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Darren Hemmings of Motive Unknown helps Sean work out why, with $600 billion a year spent on online ads, so many publications are closing. We detour into how music marketing really works.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Online advertising is a $600 billion a year industry, per Harvard Business Review.
  2. Darren says marketing is about connecting with people without treating them like a cash cow.
  3. Targeted ads mean you no longer need banner ads on music sites to reach their readers.
  4. He talks about the tactics behind chart-topping runs for Wet Leg and The 1975.
  5. The Rule of Seven describes how many touchpoints it takes to persuade someone.

Who you will hear

Darren Hemmings

Founder and managing director, Motive Unknown. Music marketing strategist who has worked with Wolf Alice, Jungle, Run The Jewels and many labels.
