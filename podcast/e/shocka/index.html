Turning Being Sectioned Into a Lifeline for Others | Shocka

7 July 2026 · 67 min

Shocka, one of the UK's leading voices on mental health in music, talks about being sectioned, losing his mum, and why vulnerability protects people better than toughness. His song of hope is Love Yourz by J. Cole, and this is the Season 1 finale.

Key takeaways

  1. Shocka was sectioned three times and later diagnosed with paranoid schizophrenia and manic depression.
  2. He wrote his book A Section of My Life so other people couldn't tell his story for him.
  3. He is a Rethink Mental Illness ambassador and has given a TEDx talk and appeared in ITV's Black Boys Can Cry.
  4. His album Vulnerability Is the New Cool was written to heal himself first and everyone else after.
