DiS004: Tune in, AI Out - the co-founders of Endel on music’s potential futures

21 February 2023 · 50 min

Endel co-founders Oleg Stavitsky and Dmitry Evgrafov talk to Sean about generative, functional music, from circadian rhythms to Brian Eno. Sean admits he is oddly optimistic about some of AI music's futures.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Endel generates music in real time to help you focus, relax, walk or sleep.
  2. Endel built an 8-hour Sleep Science playlist with Amazon Music, opening with a Kx5 track made from the hit Escape.
  3. The science behind it targets the parasympathetic nervous system to get listeners ready for sleep.
  4. Sean connects it to Sigur Ros, Bowie's Low and Max Richter's Sleep.
  5. Oleg invites listeners to join Endel's Discord community.
