Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 3

Will music survive the next 25 years? - Season 3 Prologue

16 February 2025 · 15 min

Sean opens season three by thinking out loud about what music could look like in 2050, and what has to change for it to survive streaming monopolies and squeezed venues. It is a personal start, with his own history with music and a nudge towards community as the answer.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Grassroots venues are struggling and that threatens the pipeline of new artists.
  2. Streaming giants hold a lot of power over how music gets discovered.
  3. The economics are tough for independent artists, and the gap keeps widening.
  4. Past music movements show that community has always been where change starts.
  5. The future of music depends on choices made now.
