DiS001: The trade unionist campaigning for fairer streaming - Naomi Pohl from Musicians’ Union

2 February 2023 · 37 min

Sean talks to Naomi Pohl, General Secretary of the Musicians' Union, about how we might fix streaming and the campaigns she is driving. It restarts the podcast with the question that has nagged the industry for fifteen years.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. The Musicians' Union represents 33,000 musicians and fights for fair pay and better working conditions.
  2. The Copyright (Rights and Remuneration) Bill, known as the Brennan Bill, is one route to changing the law on streaming pay.
  3. Naomi's practical tip is to find out who your local MP is and build a relationship, especially if they care about music.
  4. New members can join the MU for as little as 1 pound.
  5. The Musicians' Census is a way to count how many musicians there actually are in the UK.
