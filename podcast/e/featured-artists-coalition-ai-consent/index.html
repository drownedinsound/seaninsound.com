Nobody Consented to This: How AI Is Using Artists' Music, Voices and Likenesses Without Permission

24 March 2026 · 33 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Part 2 with David Martin asks whether AI training on artists' voices and recordings is a human rights issue, and why the UK government is courting data centres while 125,000 music workers wait. It also lands on hope, with David's answer to the £500 million question and why he thinks music in 2050 will be better than ever.

Key takeaways

  1. Record contracts signed in 2020 could not have granted consent for AI uses artists did not yet understand.
  2. David calls the deals major labels have struck with AI platforms a scandal.
  3. The Music Export Growth Scheme's whole annual budget is just £1.6 million, against government enthusiasm for AI data centres and tax breaks.
  4. The FAC and the Council of Music Makers argue that protecting human creativity is fundamental.
  5. David separates AI hype from the NFT bubble, and ends optimistic about music in 2050.
