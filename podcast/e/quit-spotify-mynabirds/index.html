Why it's time to quit Spotify

11 September 2025 · 53 min

Laura Burhenn of The Mynabirds pulled her music from Spotify after learning of Daniel Ek's investment in military AI firm Helsing. Sean explores the wave of artist departures and where people might go next.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Laura Burhenn left Spotify and posted a protest monologue after the Helsing investment.
  2. King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard and Godspeed You! Black Emperor also took music down.
  3. Individual protest can snowball into a movement.
  4. Qobuz and other alternatives come up.
  5. Artists taking part in their own devaluation is part of the bigger picture.
