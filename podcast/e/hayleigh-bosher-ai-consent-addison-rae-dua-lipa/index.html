AI & Consent Special with Addison Rae, Dua Lipa and Taylor Swift featuring Dr Hayleigh Bosher

20 May 2026 · 63 min

Helena and Sean ask copyright expert Dr Hayleigh Bosher what consent means in the age of AI, from Addison Rae's music in an ICE video to Dua Lipa suing Samsung. It starts from a Lambrini Girls clip on Channel 4 and tries to answer the questions rather than just doom-spiral.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Addison Rae may have a case against ICE for using her music without consent.
  2. Taylor Swift trademarking herself raises the question of whether that stops deepfakes.
  3. Dua Lipa is suing Samsung for £15m.
  4. The UK government's AI consultation comes up as the wider backdrop.
