Key takeaways

  1. Fans get in by showing a selfie from outside a polling place or checking voter registration.
  2. Artists are booked using data on who low-propensity voters listen to, town by town.
  3. It has to be local, and Emily launched it in 2018 with no money and an intern.
  4. Some shows are free for fans who learn about clean energy jobs.
  5. Emily also wrote How to Build a Sustainable Music Career and Collect All Revenue Streams.
