How music plays a role in US elections | Emily White, #iVoted Concerts | HMCSTW Episode 2

4 November 2026 · 49 min · with Shain Shapiro

Shain talks to Emily White, co-founder of #iVoted Concerts, about how free gigs can get low-propensity voters to the polls. Sean follows up on local listening and why free is expensive.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Fans get in by showing a selfie from outside a polling place or checking voter registration.
  2. Artists are booked using data on who low-propensity voters listen to, town by town.
  3. It has to be local, and Emily launched it in 2018 with no money and an intern.
  4. Some shows are free for fans who learn about clean energy jobs.
  5. Emily also wrote How to Build a Sustainable Music Career and Collect All Revenue Streams.
