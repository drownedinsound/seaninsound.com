The Poet Who Fights for Justice & Why She's Learning to Rest | Shareefa Energy

28 April 2026 · 56 min · with Ariana Alexander-Sefre

Poet Shareefa Energy talks to Ariana about burnout, the wave analogy that changed how she sees her place in movements, and why joy is liberation. Her song of hope is My Generation by Nas and Damian Marley.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Shareefa uses a wave analogy to think about her role in movements, which changed how she handles burnout.
  2. She argues that joy is a form of liberation, not a distraction from the fight.
  3. Her practice treats poetry as both campaigning and self-care.
  4. She has run workshops everywhere from schools to immigration detention centres, and co-produced the documentary To Kill A War Machine.
