Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 3 · Episode 26

An ethical guide to quitting Spotify

9 October 2025 · 73 min

Part two of the Spotify exodus series, with Cut Off The Spigot's Alexa Speed, artist Kadhja Bonet and returning guest Laura Burhenn of The Mynabirds. They get into why Kadhja pulled her music, what Spotify's money is funding, and what alternatives like Bandcamp and Qobuz can offer.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Kadhja Bonet explains why she removed her music from Spotify.
  2. Alexa Speed digs into the corporate influence and investments behind streaming platforms, including AI spending and Daniel Ek's defence-tech ventures.
  3. Bandcamp, Qobuz and other alternatives are discussed as practical places to listen and pay artists.
  4. The guests imagine a future where creativity and community outweigh convenience and art is valued as social good rather than for algorithmic profit.
