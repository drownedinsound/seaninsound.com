Putting the Fans First

4 November 2025 · 64 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Recorded live at Sŵn Festival in Cardiff as part of the Culture, Media and Sport Committee's Fan-Led Review of live and electronic music, with Sean introducing the panel. They borrow from football, where fans are treated as stakeholders, to ask why music treats them as consumers.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Live music generates £5.2 billion for the UK economy and employs over 210,000 people, and all of it comes from fans' pockets.
  2. The Fan-Led Review, chaired by Lord Kevin Brennan, will report to Government on fans' perspectives.
  3. The panel draws parallels between football and music, two cultures built on loyalty and community.
  4. Fans can feed in through the review's survey and the Music Fans' Voice survey.
