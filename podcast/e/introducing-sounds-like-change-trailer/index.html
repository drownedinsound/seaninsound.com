Introducing... Sounds Like Change

21 April 2026 · 2 min

A two-minute welcome to Sounds Like Change, Ariana Alexander-Sefre's podcast about how music shapes who we are, how we heal and how we change the world. It sets out the questions the series will keep coming back to.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Browse the episodes · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Each episode moves from personal stories to collective challenges and then to imagining better futures.
  2. The show asks how music affects mental health and emotional wellbeing.
  3. It asks what role artists can play in shaping a better future and how pain can become purpose.
  4. Moments of sound, reflection and creative rest are woven through each conversation.
