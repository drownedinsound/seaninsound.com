What is the function of record reviews in 2024?

27 February 2024 · 63 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Elijah, of Butterz and the Yellow Square posts, talks to Sean about curation as journalism. From pirate radio to Tumblr, we try to deconstruct the gatekeeper.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Elijah treats his Yellow Squares as a form of community-building journalism.
  2. He asks what the musical equivalent of a community note would be.
  3. He points out magazines historically sell advertising space wrapped around music coverage.
  4. He ran grime nights with Stormzy and Skepta and runs Butterz.
  5. He argues not all club closures are bad.

Who you will hear

Elijah

DJ, label founder, artist manager, Butterz. Co-founder of Butterz, pirate radio DJ and writer behind the Yellow Square posts.
