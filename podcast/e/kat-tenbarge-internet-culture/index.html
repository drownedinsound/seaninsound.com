Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 2 · Episode 7

Has music become a sub-genre of internet culture?

7 November 2023 · 59 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

NBC News tech and culture reporter Kat Tenbarge talks to Sean about the influencer economy and what the music press could learn from tech journalism. It gets into MeToo, fandoms and how virality can make or break a career.

Key takeaways

  1. Kat argues reporting that takes a point of view is what will be successful.
  2. Click-driven newsrooms push stories toward conflict, she says.
  3. She covers influencer culture, MeToo and how online mobs affect free speech.
  4. Check My Ads and Stop Funding Hate are mentioned as ways to defund hate.
  5. Her reporting includes the David Dobrik allegations and Tory Lanez trial coverage.

Who you will hear

Kat Tenbarge

Founder, Spitfire News (was NBC News tech and culture reporter at time of episode). Reporter on internet culture who has since left NBC News to run her own independent outlet, Spitfire News.
