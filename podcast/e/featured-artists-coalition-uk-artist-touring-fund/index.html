Can £125,000 Make a Difference to the Crisis in Live Music?

17 March 2026 · 48 min

Artists on the road are losing money every night while the industry reports record profits, and the Featured Artists Coalition has built a fund from a voluntary £1 levy on arena and stadium tickets to help. Sean talks to David Martin and Roxanne de Bastion about who it covers, who has opted in, and why Live Nation is the awkward question.

Key takeaways

  1. UKAT is funded by a voluntary £1 levy on arena and stadium tickets, and Phase One pays up to £7,000, covering up to 40% of tour costs.
  2. British artists' share of the global market has fallen from around 17% to 9% in nine years.
  3. Labels stopped providing tour support, and costs have climbed since 2022 while support slot fees have barely moved in decades.
  4. Touring costs are also a working-class and safety issue, with Viola Beach named as a reference point nobody has forgotten.
  5. A separate access fund covers artists with disabilities, caring responsibilities or other access needs.
