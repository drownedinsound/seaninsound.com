Sounds Like Change · SLC S1 · Episode 4

Media Bias, Gaza and the Search for Radical Empathy | Karishma Patel

27 May 2026 · 56 min

Karishma Patel followed her conscience out of the BBC after months covering Gaza, and talks to Ariana about the gap between impartiality and truth. Music runs through it all, from a piano piece composed during the genocide to Elgar's Sospiri on long walks.

Key takeaways

  1. Karishma pitched the story of Hind Rajab at the BBC and was told she was too emotionally attached.
  2. She distinguishes impartiality from truth, and is writing a book critiquing impartiality in journalism.
  3. She is an ambassador for Amnesty UK's Defend Dissent campaign and works with the Britain Palestine Media Centre.
  4. She is a classically trained pianist and opera singer, and her song of hope is Sospiri by Edward Elgar.
