Sounds Like Change · SLC S1 · Episode 6

"What's broken collectively can be fixed collectively" | Drowned in Sound founder Sean Adams

25 June 2026 · 76 min · with Ariana Alexander-Sefre

The pilot, with Drowned in Sound founder Sean Adams choosing Johann Johannsson's Fordlandia as his song of hope. It runs from Dead Prez and a Dorset childhood to why individual wellness can't replace systemic fixes. Recorded as a pilot, Ariana decided it was too good not to publish.

Key takeaways

  1. Dead Prez's Let's Get Free was the record that unlocked something bigger for Sean at sixteen.
  2. He argues for systemic fixes, like chill-out rooms and therapy funded as salary, over individual coping mechanisms.
  3. He points to Universal Music giving staff mental health days while freelancers absorb the extra work.
  4. He talks about the StopTheTories tactical voting campaign and working with Max Richter and Yulia Mahr on Voices.
