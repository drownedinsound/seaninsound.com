Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 5 · Episode 2

Our 2026 predictions: New Acts, Big comebacks, Gig ticket laws, and more

6 January 2026 · 46 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Sean and Emma look into the crystal ball and the release schedules for 2026. Tipping acts, the lack of shared musical moments, anxiety around AI, and whether ticketing and grassroots funding might shift power back to scenes.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. They tip acts who should break through and name the corporate barriers in the way.
  2. There's a strange absence of shared musical moments.
  3. Anxiety around AI music and falling trust in big tech platforms are the forces under the surface.
  4. Changes to ticketing, touring and grassroots funding may rebalance money towards scenes.
