Geoff Barrow (Portishead) Made a Film with a Sleaford Mod and Every Penny Invada Had

16 June 2026 · 68 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Sean sits in the sun outside Geoff Barrow's home to talk about GAME, a 1993 rave-era rural thriller starring Jason Williamson of Sleaford Mods, made for £150,000 which was nearly everything Invada had. It runs from Drive and Mica Levi to what £500 million would do.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. GAME was shot for about £150,000, essentially all of Invada's money.
  2. It started as a reaction to being told a film couldn't be made without the right connections.
  3. Geoff stripped the score back after years of watching low-budget films drown in music.
  4. DJ Smudge made the period-accurate 1993 tracks for the soundtrack.
