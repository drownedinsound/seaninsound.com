Enter Shikari exclusive: Rou Reynolds on why the band goes dormant on 31 December 2027. And what comes next.

2 November 2026 · 86 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Rou Reynolds talks about why Enter Shikari will go dormant on 31 December 2027, weeks after the announcement. He is open about anxiety, panic attacks and OCD, and about finding his footing again.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. The band falls dormant as a recording and performing entity on 31 December 2027, and it is not a goodbye tour.
  2. Rou describes anxiety, insomnia, panic attacks on stage and OCD in early 2026.
  3. He has been experimenting with giving 50% rather than 100% on stage.
  4. The band began at a youth club and Rou calls it a hobby that got out of hand.
  5. Enter Shikari were the first to give £1 per UK ticket to Music Venue Trust.
