What’s it like to broadcast from a war zone?

4 February 2024 · 41 min

Award-winning reporter Julia MacFarlane explains what music media can learn from international news. We talk Beirut, Brexit and recording events accurately.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Julia says recording events accurately and impartially needs trained professionals.
  2. She has reported for the BBC and ABC News on major global events.
  3. She co-hosts the One Decision podcast with former MI6 chief Sir Richard Dearlove.
  4. Self-shot journalism is hard but rewarding.
  5. Personal stories make complex global issues relatable.
