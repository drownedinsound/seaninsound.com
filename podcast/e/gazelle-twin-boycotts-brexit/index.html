When Boycotts Work: Arms, Hope and Soundtracking Brexit with Gazelle Twin

19 February 2026 · 56 min

Elizabeth Bernholz of Gazelle Twin talks about withdrawing from her Kings Place residency over an arms-industry conference, and the venue cancelling it eleven days later. They also cover Pastoral, her Brexit-era album that DiS gave 10/10, and performing unmasked.

Key takeaways

  1. In May 2025 she withdrew from Kings Place over a Lockheed Martin-sponsored conference, which was cancelled after 1,200+ artists and fans signed an open letter.
  2. She explains practical tactics of organising a boycott.
  3. PRS Foundation funding enabled some of her most political work.
  4. She talks about film scoring as financial stability and Ireland's basic income for musicians.
