Music Industry Misconduct: Why The System Still Protects Abusers

16 October 2025 · 74 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Sean and Emma Wilkes are joined by Sophie K and Yasmin from the podcast On Wednesdays We Wear Black to ask what accountability looks like in music, and why it still lags decades behind. They cover Marilyn Manson, Chris Brown, Brand New and the long normalisation of abuse in classic rock.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. The group looks at how power, money and silence decide who gets forgiven and who doesn't, from Marilyn Manson to Chris Brown and Brand New.
  2. They discuss the Look Away documentary and The Guardian's review of it as a picture of long-normalised abuse in classic rock.
  3. The conversation asks when 'sorry' stops counting, and what justice looks like without a working system.
  4. They question the media's role in reckoning, and the limits of online moralism and tokenism in festival representation.
