What Great Music Journalists Hear That Algorithms Miss: DiS meets Ilana Kaplan

5 August 2025 · 59 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Sean talks to Ilana Kaplan, music journalist, about what human curation catches that algorithms miss. She tells the Billie Eilish and Zola Jesus stories and why trust makes better journalism.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Ilana spotted Billie Eilish at an early Mercury Lounge show.
  2. She got Zola Jesus into Vogue by selling leftfield artists to mainstream editors.
  3. The boygenius desert cover shoot was self-funded to make something viral.
  4. Trust with artists makes better journalism than aggressive questioning.
  5. Honest reporting can get you blacklisted by PR.
