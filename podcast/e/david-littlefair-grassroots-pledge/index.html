The Grassroots Pledge: Wrestling Instagram, Spotify, and the Arts Council with promoter David Littlefair

24 February 2026 · 71 min

Promoter David Littlefair of Marrapalooza explains the grassroots pledge: pulling ad spend from Meta and not booking on follower counts. They get into Arts Council funding, Spotify and community-owned venues.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. The pledge redirects ad spend away from Meta, refuses follower-count booking and puts money back into the local scene.
  2. 85% of Arts Council music funding goes to opera and classical, with brass bands and folk at 0.6% each.
  3. There is a risk the grassroots levy gets offshored by Instagram ads.
  4. He makes a case for community-owned venues modelled on working men's clubs.
