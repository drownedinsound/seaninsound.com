Introducing... How Music Can Save The World

27 October 2026 · 5 min · with Shain Shapiro

A five minute trailer in which Shain Shapiro introduces his new series, a companion to his second book. It aims to show how music can help with development, health, climate, crisis and recovery, and collective morality.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. The series is a companion to Shain's second book of the same name.
  2. The book makes a case for music across five areas: economic development, healthcare, climate, crisis and recovery, and collective morality.
  3. Shain was a contributor to Drowned in Sound before working with mayors and the UN.
  4. Sean produces the series and appears at the end of each episode.
