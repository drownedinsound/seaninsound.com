UK Caps Ticket Resale at Face Value: What Took So Long?

19 November 2025 · 56 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

The UK Government announced that ticket resale above face value will be made illegal, and Adam Webb of FanFair Alliance joins Sean to unpack it. They trace the long campaign behind it and what it might mean for fans, artists and venues.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Resale above face value is to be made illegal, with strict limits on service fees and new enforcement powers.
  2. Resale platforms' mark-ups are estimated to cost fans around £112 million a year.
  3. The pressure built over years through Trading Standards, the CMA, tabloid exposes, Ed Sheeran's court cases and evidence from managers, artists and unions.
  4. Webb is a central figure in the campaign through FanFair Alliance.
