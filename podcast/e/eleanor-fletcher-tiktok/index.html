DiS003: TikTok, ”Authenticity” and Music with Eleanor from Crystal Fighters

16 February 2023 · 50 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Eleanor Fletcher of Crystal Fighters explains how she built a big TikTok audience in a few months, and Sean tries to get his head around what you are meant to do on it. It is a mini masterclass that makes the whole thing feel a lot less daunting.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. The first few seconds of a video are the hook, and that matters more than a pop melody.
  2. Eleanor's rule is to be interesting and intriguing.
  3. Keep a running list of video ideas in your notes app.
  4. She built roughly 900k likes on the platform in just a few months.
  5. This is part of a sub-series on the foundations of where the music industry is at.
