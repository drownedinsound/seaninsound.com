How We Open Music’s Doors to Everyone (Not Just the Rich)

16 February 2025 · 46 min

Sean talks to Matt Griffiths, CEO of Youth Music, about who gets to make music and who gets quietly shut out. Early support shapes careers, and the episode asks whether access is a right or a privilege.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Early support through youth-led projects and grassroots funding shapes who gets to make music.
  2. Working-class musicians face financial, social and industry barriers that are often hidden.
  3. Ezra Collective credited early organisations for their Mercury Prize win.
  4. Youth Music is a UK charity funding grassroots projects for young musicians.
  5. Rising costs and funding cuts are turning music into a luxury.
