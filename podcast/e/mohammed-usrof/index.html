Climate Justice, Occupation & The Future We Are Fighting For | Mohammed Usrof

12 May 2026 · 54 min · with Ariana Alexander-Sefre

Mohammed Usrof of the Palestinian Institute for Climate Strategy asks what it means to imagine a future while the collapse is still happening. His song of hope is Spoils by Massive Attack featuring Hope Sandoval.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Usrof talks about energy sovereignty under occupation and why it matters for climate justice.
  2. He makes the case for fossil fuel embargoes.
  3. He argues that art, politics and liberation can never be disentangled.
  4. He is co-founder and Executive Director of PICS and a PhD candidate researching fossil fuel histories and refineries in Palestine.
