What If You Could Taste Music? kwes. on his "dreamy" new LP on Warp

3 March 2026 · 52 min

Kwes. talks about burnout after fifteen years of producing for others and the spilled-water moment that led to his new Warp album Kinds. Every track is named after a colour from his synaesthesia.

Key takeaways

  1. A drink his daughter knocked over mid-drawing became the blueprint for the record.
  2. Kinds was made in six months of late-night flow states.
  3. Tracks are named after colours he experiences through synaesthesia.
  4. He discusses producing for others versus his own work, and burnout recovery.
