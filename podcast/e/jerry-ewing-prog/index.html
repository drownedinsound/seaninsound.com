The joys of magazine-making with PROG Editor Jerry Ewing

4 February 2024 · 62 min

PROG editor Jerry Ewing talks to Sean about going from a Marillion-inspired fanzine to Classic Rock, Metal Hammer and PROG. We ask what running a genre magazine is like now.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Jerry started with a fanzine called Court Jester before moving to pro magazines.
  2. He argues progressive music is defined by ideas and approach rather than sound.
  3. Know your readers, he says; be comfortable with them and they will be with you.
  4. He speculates print might have a vinyl-style resurgence.
  5. Communication between human beings is at the root of journalism.

Who you will hear

Jerry Ewing

Editor, PROG Magazine. Editor of PROG, previously at Classic Rock and Metal Hammer.
