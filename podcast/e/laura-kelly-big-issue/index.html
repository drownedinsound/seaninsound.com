Is music journalism’s future in campaigning?

23 October 2023 · 50 min

Laura Kelly, Culture Editor at The Big Issue, explains how Venue Watch uses journalism to help save grassroots venues. We ask how music writing can build a bridge between the art and the world around it.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Laura says more than two UK venues were closing every single week.
  2. Venue Watch tells the story of a venue each week to drive action.
  3. She sees music journalism as building a bridge between the art, the artist and the world.
  4. Only 16% of people in creative jobs are from working class backgrounds, according to the Policy and Evidence Centre.
  5. The Big Issue gives homeless and vulnerably housed people a way to earn an income.
