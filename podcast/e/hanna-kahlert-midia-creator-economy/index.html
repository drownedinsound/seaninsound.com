Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 3 · Episode 34

Can music still cut through in 2026? DiS meets a leading researcher

27 December 2025 · 71 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Hanna Kahlert of MIDiA Research joins Sean to ask why musicians now face the pressures of YouTubers and streamers without the same protections. They look at the content-creation time sink and why likes and views so rarely become income.

Key takeaways

  1. Musicians increasingly work like creators but without the tools, protections or paths to sustainability.
  2. Constant content creation is a time sink and success metrics have become distorted.
  3. Discovery is easier and more exhausting, with 'lean back' listening and 'lean through' fandom.
  4. Likes, views and viral moments often fail to translate into income or longevity.
