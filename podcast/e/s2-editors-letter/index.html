Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 2 · Episode 1

Introduction to S2: The Editor’s Letter / Voice Note

23 October 2023 · 13 min

Sean introduces season two as an editor's letter crossed with a meandering voice memo, asking where music journalism is headed. To mark 23 years of DiS he sets out to talk to TikTokkers, rock writers and tech reporters.
