Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 2 · Episode 13

Why Music Journalism Matters

4 February 2024 · 78 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

The Quietus co-founder John Doran reflects on Pitchfork's demise and whether music journalism is destined to be a hobby. It ranges from Fela Kuti to Sophie to the Last Dinner Party.

Key takeaways

  1. John compares writing about music to composing poetry about the weather.
  2. Fela Kuti widened his horizons and led to a Guardian trip to Cairo.
  3. They discuss search engine optimisation shaping what music media commissions.
  4. They look at the industry plant nonsense around The Last Dinner Party.
  5. The question is whether reader funding can sustain music writing.
