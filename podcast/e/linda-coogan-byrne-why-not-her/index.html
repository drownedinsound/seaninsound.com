The Report That Shook the Music Industry & Exposed Inequality

16 March 2025 · 69 min

Sean talks to Linda Coogan Byrne, the campaigner and music consultant behind Why Not Her?, about the radio airplay reports that exposed gender and racial gaps. Her point is that data validates lived experience, and that is why it moves an industry.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Why Not Her? reports exposed gender and racial disparities in radio airplay.
  2. The industry's first reactions were denial, excuses and pushback.
  3. Data validates lived experiences and holds power to account.
  4. Whether the industry has really improved or is paying lip service is still an open question.
  5. Linda recommends Mona Eltahawy, Emma Dabiri and Mary Beard.
