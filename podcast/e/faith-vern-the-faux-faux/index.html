DiS002: Discover... The Faux Faux (Faith Vern from PINS)

9 February 2023 · 35 min

Faith Vern from PINS talks about The Faux Faux, her new solo project, whose debut single is the first release on the rebooted DiS Singles Club. Sean asks how it differs from the party punk of PINS and how Plath and leaving Manchester fed into the track.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. The Faux Faux's debut single is the first release on the rebooted Drowned in Sound Singles Club.
  2. Faith has collaborated with Iggy Pop and Maxine Peake and played main stages like Reading with PINS.
  3. She talks about writing music for HBO, Netflix and Apple TV shows.
  4. Sylvia Plath and moving out of Manchester shaped the track.
  5. The video was shot in the North of England on 500ft of film.

Who you will hear

Faith Vern

Musician, The Faux Faux / PINS. Manchester musician from PINS, releasing solo music as The Faux Faux.
