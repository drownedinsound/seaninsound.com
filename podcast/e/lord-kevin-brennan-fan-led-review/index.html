What can music fans expect from the PM Andy Burnham era? DiS meets Lord Brennan

25 July 2026 · 61 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Lord Kevin Brennan led the fan-led review of live music, and Sean asks what has happened since and what a music-loving prime minister like Andy Burnham might do. They talk about the 4,144 responses, the Fans' Charter and community-owned venues.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. 4,144 fans responded, more than any committee inquiry has recorded.
  2. The Fans' Charter has seven essentials: celebration, ticketing, grassroots, safety, accessibility, transport and voice.
  3. Andy Burnham is a shareholder in Music Venue Properties, which has taken venues like The Snug in Atherton into community ownership.
  4. Brennan is also a musician and has released a solo album.
