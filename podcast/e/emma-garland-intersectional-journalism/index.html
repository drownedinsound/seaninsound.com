Shouldn’t all music journalism be intersectional?

5 November 2023 · 56 min

Writer Emma Garland talks to Sean about music writing that covers far more of life than a 50-word review. We start with the difference between journalism, culture writing and criticism, then spiral off.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Emma separates journalism, culture writing and music criticism.
  2. Interviewees are more guarded now because culture war means they speak to far more than their fans.
  3. She worries about limited career pathways for emerging writers.
  4. She is writing Tell All Your Friends, a cultural history of mainstream emo from 2000 to 2013.
  5. Shoutouts include Sirin Kale, Craig Jenkins and Zing Tsjeng.

Who you will hear

Emma Garland

Writer, editor, journalist, Freelance (Vice, Huck, The Quietus, Dazed). Writer and editor who has covered music and culture for Vice, Huck, The Face and more.
