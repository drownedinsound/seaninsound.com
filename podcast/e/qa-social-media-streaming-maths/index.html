How to Use Social Media When You Hate It & Why Streaming Math Ain't Mathin' - Q&A Special

29 July 2025 · 36 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Sean answers listener questions about using social media when you hate it and why streaming maths does not add up. One number sticks: 25 million streams over a decade might pay about £6,000 a year.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Social media can be reframed as an extension of artistic practice.
  2. 25 million streams over a decade might earn about £6,000 a year, less than minimum wage.
  3. Streaming numbers rarely translate into live audiences.
  4. Live streaming only works when a performance is designed for it.
  5. Platforms that allow direct fan communication beat vanity metrics.
