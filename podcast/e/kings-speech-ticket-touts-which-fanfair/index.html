Ticket Touts Rejoice! DiS & Which? React to The King's Speech

14 May 2026 · 40 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

The King's Speech arrived without a Ticketing Bill, just a draft buried on page 64 of a supplementary document. Sean walks through sixteen years of the fight, then Kat Cereda of Which? and Adam Webb of FanFair Alliance say what comes next.

Key takeaways

  1. What arrived was a draft bill, not legislation, so expect more consultation and an uncertain timeline.
  2. Resale platforms helping shape the law is exactly what campaigners feared.
  3. The fight runs from Sharon Hodgson's Private Member's Bill in 2010 through the Waterson Review, FanFair in 2016 and the Oasis dynamic pricing row to November 2025.
  4. Adam Webb has been in the campaign since 2016 and gives his reaction at the end.
