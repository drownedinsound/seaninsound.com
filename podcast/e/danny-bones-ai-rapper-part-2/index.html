Meet The AI Rapper Beloved By The Far-Right - Part 2

8 September 2026 · 43 min

Part 2 moves on to what Danny Bones means for music: a far-right AI rapper borrowing from Black music, the response from musicians, and what happens when creative work can be copied at scale. It ends with what fans can do.

Key takeaways

  1. More than a third of new Apple Music uploads are now AI-generated, according to Music Ally.
  2. Australia has banned fully AI-generated songs from the ARIA charts.
  3. Spotify has introduced an AI persona label and keeps AI artists out of default recommendations.
  4. The devaluing of human creative labour and copyright come up.
