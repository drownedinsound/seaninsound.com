Show notes

In part two of our conversation with journalists Effie Webb and Harriet Williamson, we move beyond who created Danny Bones and ask what his existence means for music itself.

We discuss the irony of a far-right AI rapper borrowing from Black music, the response from musicians, and what happens when technology can imitate creative work at scale with little to no consequence.

We get into copyright, the devaluing of human creative labour, Spotify’s response to AI artists, whether Danny Bones is an outlier or an early sign of what’s coming, and finally, what music fans can do to help musicians, songwriters and artists to remain at the centre of music.

This week's playlist is songs that give you a bit of pride in where you are from. Amongst the picks, Harriet chose 'White Man in Hammersmith Palais' by The Clash and Effie chose 'LDN' by Lily Allen, two London songs, one of them about race and punk and reggae and the other about what the city actually looks like underneath, which is a fairly pointed answer to a record trading on national pride.

About the guests

Effie Webb

AI reporter, The Bureau of Investigative Journalism

Effie Webb is a reporter at The Bureau of Investigative Journalism, where she covers artificial intelligence, political influence and online harms. Before joining the Bureau she covered AI at Business Insider, reporting on the workforce that trains and tests AI systems. Her March 2026 investigation into an AI-generated rapper promoted with money from a far-right party was among the first of its kind.
