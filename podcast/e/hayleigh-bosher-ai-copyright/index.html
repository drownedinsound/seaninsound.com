DiS007: AI, AI, AI! UK Government’s War on Copyrighted Music?! Beatles AI song! - Dr Hayleigh Bosher explains it all

21 March 2023 · 59 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Dr Hayleigh Bosher, an expert in intellectual property law, helps Sean work out whether we should worry about the UK government letting AI firms access copyrighted music more easily. We also get into the bigger question of AI making new music from old.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. The spring 2023 budget signalled easier access to copyrighted material for AI firms.
  2. Not everything fed into AI has gone in with permission, let alone consent.
  3. Hayleigh is author of Copyright in the Music Industry and a senior lecturer at Brunel.
  4. She hosts the Whose Song Is It Anyway? podcast.
  5. The episode touches on the Daddy's Car Beatles-style AI track and Jeremy Hunt's AI Sandbox.

Who you will hear

Dr Hayleigh Bosher

Reader in IP Law, Brunel University London. Reader in Intellectual Property Law at Brunel Law School and author of Copyright in the Music Industry.
