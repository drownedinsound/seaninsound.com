Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 4 · Episode 2

The Stories of 2025 - Part 1: Megagigs, Grassroots, and AI slop

15 December 2025 · 38 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Sean and Emma count down stories five and four of the year: mega-gigs set against a squeezed grassroots, then AI moving from theory to politics and law. The question running through it is what growth is for if the foundations are cracking.

Key takeaways

  1. Record-breaking mega-gigs sit alongside artists at every other level cancelling tours.
  2. Taylor Swift-scale touring drives economic growth while grassroots venues are under existential pressure.
  3. AI in 2025 stopped being theoretical and demanded political, legal and cultural responses.
  4. They discuss consent, transparency and 'human-made' music labelling.
