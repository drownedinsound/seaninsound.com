Synths, Sound & Music’s Future with Martyn Ware (The Human League / Heaven 17 / BEF / Electronically Yours podcast)

12 April 2025 · 31 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Recorded at Tallinn Music Week, Sean talks to Martyn Ware of The Human League and Heaven 17 about what the future sounded like when synths arrived. It moves from Sheffield DIY to immersive sound installations and what comes next with AI.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Martyn Ware co-founded The Human League and Heaven 17 and produced Tina Turner and Erasure.
  2. Sheffield DIY culture gave him creative confidence that lasted.
  3. His career moved from the charts to immersive audio installations.
  4. His podcast Electronically Yours preserves the legacy of sound.
  5. He is optimistic about what comes next, while aware of dystopian risks.

Who you will hear

Martyn Ware

Musician, producer, sound artist, The Human League / Heaven 17 / Illustrious Company. Martyn Ware is a founding member of The Human League and Heaven 17 and creator of immersive sound installations.
