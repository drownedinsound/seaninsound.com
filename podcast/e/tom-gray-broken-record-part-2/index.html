Gomez's Tom Gray: How to Fight Spotify in Parliament and Why One Difficult Conversation a Day Is Enough - Part 2

1 July 2026 · 58 min

Part 2 is the fight: how Tom Gray's lockdown tweet turned into the #BrokenRecord campaign and the Brennan Bill, and where it keeps hitting walls. The last twenty minutes are about hope and what Spotify's Joe Rogan money could do for songwriters instead.

Key takeaways

  1. UK copyright law was last substantially reformed in 1988, and performers have no equitable remuneration for streams.
  2. No regulation makes platforms record songwriter data at upload, which invites fraud.
  3. Tom calls the AI consultation a failure and describes a copyright gang and a tech gang inside Whitehall.
  4. He ends on a per diem proposal for songwriters.
