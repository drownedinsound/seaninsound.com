Offline Joy: The Do-It-Together Spirit Fueling Stealing Sheep’s New Era

30 March 2025 · 59 min

Sean talks to Becky Hawley of Stealing Sheep about their new label G-IRL and fifteen years of doing it together. It is about offline joy, Liverpool and building something real while tech tries to flatten everything.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Stealing Sheep launched their own label, G-IRL (Girl In Real Life).
  2. Community and offline spaces matter more than algorithms.
  3. Liverpool's scene shaped their creative growth over fifteen years.
  4. They see room for utopian possibilities in AI and future tech, as well as fears.
  5. Artists need to unlearn some old rules about how to be in a band.
