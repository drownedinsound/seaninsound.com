DiS005: How music can embrace the power of AI with ChatGPT expert David Boyle

22 February 2023 · 54 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Audience expert David Boyle, co-author of PROMPT, shows Sean how musicians can get better answers out of ChatGPT. Mostly it comes down to writing better prompts.

Key takeaways

  1. If ChatGPT does not impress you at first, David's advice is to assume your prompt was not good enough.
  2. Sean uses it to find films by the songs on their soundtracks and to decode contract legalese.
  3. David and team wrote a new edition of PROMPT for musicians after this conversation.
  4. The episode links the Hannah Peel tweet that inspired it and Nick Cave's blog post on ChatGPT.
  5. Water & Music's deep dive on AI and music is flagged as further reading.

Who you will hear

David Boyle

Founder and director, Audience Strategies. Founder of the data and audience consultancy Audience Strategies and co-author of PROMPT.
