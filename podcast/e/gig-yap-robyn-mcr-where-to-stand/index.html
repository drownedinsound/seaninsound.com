Gig Yap: Robyn, My Chemical Romance, Matt Berninger and Los Campesinos!, and Where to Stand

15 August 2026 · 56 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Sean and Helena catch up on gigs: Robyn at the O2, My Chemical Romance's Black Parade at Wembley, Matt Berninger and Los Campesinos! at the Electric Ballroom. They also work out where to stand and why obstructed view seats can be the best.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

Key takeaways

  1. Helena has worked out where to stand in most London venues if you're under five foot five.
  2. Obstructed view seats can turn out to be the best in the house.
  3. The episode is a format experiment and listeners are invited to send feedback.
  4. Robyn sold out the O2 without being an obvious household name.
