Is music finally ready to confront misogyny? DiS meets CheerUpLuv

25 September 2025 · 62 min

Sean talks to Eliza Hatch of Cheer Up Luv about her Glamour investigation into misogyny in music, which found over half of women in the industry have faced discrimination. They ask what accountability could look like.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. More than half of women in the music industry have faced discrimination.
  2. Government reform has stalled, and NDAs and libel laws help keep abuse quiet.
  3. Arena tours by Chris Brown and Marilyn Manson raise the art versus artist debate.
  4. Lambrini Girls and Nova Twins reacted to the stats.
  5. Smashing the patriarchy is good for men too.
