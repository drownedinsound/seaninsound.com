Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 6 · Episode 11

Why Your Favourite Band's Fans Might Not Be Real

7 July 2026 · 79 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Hanna Kahlert of MIDiA Research joins Sean for a mid-year check-in, spending most of it on the clipping economy where agencies build fan accounts for roughly a dollar per thousand views. It also touches AI, spatial audio, third spaces and smart glasses.

Key takeaways

  1. Eliza McLamb's Fake Fans essay and stories in Wired, New York Magazine and The Verge sparked the debate.
  2. A crowd full of phones can mean a growing audience or nobody real in the room.
  3. Hanna mentors at Abbey Road Red, working with founders on spatial audio and an Airbnb for recording studios.
  4. Pubs, youth clubs and other third spaces matter for music.

Who you will hear

Hanna Kahlert

Senior social analyst, MIDiA Research. Covers social media and the creator economy, and mentors at Abbey Road Red.
