Protect grassroots music, save so much more. A chat with Enter Shikari’s Rou Reynolds

2 September 2025 · 26 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Backstage at Reading, Sean and Emma Wilkes talk to Rou Reynolds of Enter Shikari about the £1 grassroots venue levy and choosing which crisis gets the spotlight. Supporting small venues is community organising, not charity.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. The £1 levy on arena tickets supports small venues.
  2. St Albans' scene prepared the band for mainstream success.
  3. Supporting grassroots is community organising, not charity.
  4. Music can be an antidote to neoliberal isolation.
  5. Rou chose to speak about Gaza at Reading: to be silent in times of atrocity is to assist it.
