Flying Rivers, Slipknot Swifts & Musical Frogs: Take This Podcast For A Walk In Nature

1 January 2026 · 67 min

A New Year special backstage at EarthSonic Live in Manchester Museum, where swifts, frogs and flying rivers meet music. Sean talks to RSPB conservationists, Tori Tsui, Warp's Hannah Overton and artists back from COP30.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. RSPB conservationists explain why swifts are disappearing and what red squirrels say about biodiversity loss.
  2. Hinako Omori samples frogs in the museum's Vivarium.
  3. Four members of FLOW reflect on taking droughts, floods and flying rivers to COP30 in Belem as hip-hop, spoken word and song.
  4. Tori Tsui shares how music became central to her campaigning.
