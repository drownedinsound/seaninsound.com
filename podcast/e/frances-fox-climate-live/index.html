Festivals For Future: Frances Fox on Music’s Role in Climate Justice

23 February 2025 · 66 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Sean talks to Frances Fox, founder of Climate Live, about why festivals are such good places to start climate conversations. They get into greenwashing, touring footprints and a solar-powered pink bus.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Festivals are at risk from extreme weather, so the industry has a stake in climate action.
  2. Meeting people where they are, like at a festival, engages the unengaged.
  3. Touring, vinyl and streaming all carry hidden environmental costs.
  4. Climate Live's solar-powered pink bus takes climate justice to festival crowds.
  5. Artists, venues and labels can all push for real change instead of greenwashing.

Who you will hear

Frances Fox

Founder, Climate Live. Frances Fox founded Climate Live and was a leader in the UK youth climate strikes.
