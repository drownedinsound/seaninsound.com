How Music Supports Mental Health with Ariana Alexander-Sefre (SPOKE)

22 June 2025 · 54 min

Sean talks to Ariana Alexander-Sefre, co-founder of SPOKE, about whether music can sit alongside therapy. They ask why the industry treats artists as products when they often act as caregivers.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. SPOKE has worked with over 100 artists to embed CBT and mindfulness techniques in lyrics and melodies.
  2. Music can help people metabolise emotion.
  3. Artists carry an emotional toll that the industry rarely supports.
  4. There is a case for artists to be a recognised part of healthcare.
  5. Music Minds Matter and Sally Anne Gross's work are mentioned as further reading.
