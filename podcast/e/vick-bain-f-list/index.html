DiS006: Women’s Equality in Music & Glastonbury’s pipeline with Vick Bain from The F List / ISM

8 March 2023 · 51 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

For International Women's Day, Sean talks to Vick Bain, founder of The F-List and President of the ISM, about her PhD research into inequality in music. We get into the Ivors' pitiful number of women winners and Glastonbury's so-called pipeline problem.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Vick's research found very few women among Ivors songwriting award winners.
  2. She set up the F-List, a database of female+ musicians.
  3. Glastonbury's Emily Eavis described difficulty finding non-male headliners as a pipeline problem.
  4. Vick appeared on Woman's Hour and Newsnight in the days before the episode.
  5. WeAreMusic lists support links for anyone affected by harassment in the industry.

Who you will hear

Vick Bain

Founder of The F-List; ISM President 2022-23, The F-List / Independent Society of Musicians. Researcher and campaigner who founded the F-List directory and is President of the ISM.
