Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 3 · Episode 25

Drowned in Sound is 25 today: Big lessons from the past and a new era unfurls

2 October 2025 · 63 min

Sean sits down with DiS staff writer Emma Wilkes to mark 25 years of Drowned in Sound, and they end up swapping the questions usually kept for guests. It starts with Emma's Jeremy Corbyn interview about grassroots venues and ends up in how music and politics keep leaking into each other, and what music might look like in 2050.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Emma's interview with Jeremy Corbyn about grassroots venues was the way into a bigger conversation about why music and politics can't be pulled apart.
  2. Music can be a gateway into politics, and politics can just as easily lead people back to music.
  3. Sean answers the question of how he would spend the $450m Spotify gave Joe Rogan, and how money could flow into culture instead.
  4. They talk about the pressure on artists, publicists and journalists, including mental health and addiction in the industry, and point to Music Minds Matter.
  5. They look ahead to music in 2050 and what comes next for the DiS website, newsletter and podcast.
