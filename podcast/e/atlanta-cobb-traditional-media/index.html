Is ’Traditional Media’ Still Relevant for New Artists?

23 November 2023 · 59 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Music business coach Atlanta Cobb asks Sean whether new artists still need the music press in the age of TikTok. She started on a Florence + The Machine fan site and now coaches hundreds of artists.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Atlanta says the industry's TikTok metrics focus overlooks talented artists without big followings.
  2. Consistency matters for how press champions artists and how she advises hers.
  3. She started as a Florence + The Machine fan site creator.
  4. She has worked at Island and Columbia Records and managed Becky Hill, Ella Henderson and Camden Cox.
  5. Fan communities are central to promotion now.

Who you will hear

Atlanta Cobb

Music consultant, business coach, Music Industry Mentor. Coach and former manager at Crown Talent & Media Group, formerly at Island and Columbia.
