How Lore, DIY Music Scenes & The Cure Inspired This Viral Tiktokker (And What Musicians Can Learn)

12 August 2025 · 42 min

Sean talks to Royce, aka ShoshinBoy, the TikTok creator making music history videos with VHS cameras and rotary phones. It asks how DIY ethics can survive on corporate platforms.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Primary sources and context beat Wikipedia aggregation.
  2. His analogue setup is an anti-gimmick gimmick that grew a real community.
  3. A Nirvana video reached 2 million views.
  4. The creator economy extracts, and community service is the alternative.
  5. Nostalgia both preserves and destroys cultural memory.
