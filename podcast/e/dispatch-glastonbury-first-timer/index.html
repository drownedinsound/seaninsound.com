DiSpatch: What a Glastonbury First-Timer Found - Hope, Love, Unity, Resistance & Joy

5 July 2025 · 37 min

Emma Wilkes goes to Glastonbury for the first time and sends back a sonic diary, with interviews at Terminal 1 and vox pops from the crowd. It catches the politics of the weekend too, including Amyl and the Sniffers.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

Key takeaways

  1. Emma Wilkes compares the real festival to the coverage she had seen before.
  2. Terminal 1 and Laima Layton feature in an interview.
  3. Politically charged moments, including Amyl and the Sniffers, stood out.
  4. Strangers share their expectations versus reality.
  5. In Place Of War's GRRRL gets a mention.
