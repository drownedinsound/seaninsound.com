Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 3 · Episode 12

DiSpatch: What a Glastonbury First-Timer Found - Hope, Love, Unity, Resistance & Joy

5 July 2025 · 37 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Emma Wilkes goes to Glastonbury for the first time and sends back a sonic diary, with interviews at Terminal 1 and vox pops from the crowd. It catches the politics of the weekend too, including Amyl and the Sniffers.

Key takeaways

  1. Emma Wilkes compares the real festival to the coverage she had seen before.
