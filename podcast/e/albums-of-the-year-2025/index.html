Albums of the Year: Emma Wilkes & Sean Adams Pick Their Standouts

8 December 2025 · 50 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Emma Wilkes and Sean pick the albums that stuck in 2025, with no claim to a definitive list. They also talk about the collapse of monoculture and why criticism still matters in a year of fractured scenes.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · Meet the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. The list is the records that demanded repeat listens, not a ranking.
  2. They discuss the collapse of monoculture and the impossibility of keeping up.
  3. Criticism still matters amid algorithmic bubbles and an overwhelming volume of new music.
  4. A companion playlist is curated on Qobuz.
