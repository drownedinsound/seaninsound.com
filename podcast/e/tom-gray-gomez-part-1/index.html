How Gomez's Tom Gray Ended Up Taking On Spotify in Parliament - Part 1

23 June 2026 · 56 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Sean finally sits down with Tom Gray to talk about making Bring It On in bedrooms, winning the Mercury over Massive Attack and Pulp, and chasing down Tim Buckley. It is the music-first half before the Spotify fight.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Bring It On was recorded in bedrooms and back rooms when the band were 22 and 23.
  2. The limits of home recording shaped Gomez's early sound.
  3. Tom spent years chasing Tim Buckley rarities, and one track, Dolphins, made it worthwhile.
  4. He now scores CBeebies shows, including Tilly and Friends.

Who you will hear

Tom Gray

Songwriter, founding member of Gomez, Chair of The Ivors Academy, Gomez / Ivors Academy / Broken Record. Mercury Prize winner who founded the #BrokenRecord campaign on streaming pay.
