A Goth Walked Into Nature To Make a Radio Show. A Zine Walked Out.

23 March 2025 · 73 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Sean talks with the creatives behind the Fresh Hell zine, where goth culture meets the natural world. It started with a Gothic Disco radio show recorded at Walthamstow Wetlands and asks how creativity can shift the climate story.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Fresh Hell uses goth culture to reframe our relationship with nature.
  2. The zine grew out of Freya Beer's Gothic Disco show at Walthamstow Wetlands.
  3. Creativity can move people from awe of nature to cultural change.
  4. Purpose Disruptors is a network of 5,000+ creatives pushing advertising to align with climate goals.
  5. Musicians, media and fans all have a part in changing the climate narrative.

Who you will hear

Comms & Engagement Manager, Purpose Disruptors. Aimee Brewerton works on climate-focused storytelling and cultural transformation at Purpose Disruptors.
