Drowned in Sound Podcast · Season 3 · Episode 14

How to Start a DIY Music Business Before You Feel Ready - Girls Twiddling Knobs on Mission Driven Music Careers

22 July 2025 · 60 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Sean talks to Isobel Anderson of Girls Twiddling Knobs about starting a mission-driven music business before you feel ready. Feeling like an outsider turns out to be an advantage.

Key takeaways

  1. Starting before you feel ready is part of building something lasting.
  2. Isobel moved from singer-songwriter to a PhD in Sonic Arts and over 25 million Spotify streams.
  3. Girls Twiddling Knobs grew from a Facebook group into courses, community and a podcast.
  4. Multiple revenue streams are now reality for musicians.
  5. Imposter feelings can sit alongside real qualification.
