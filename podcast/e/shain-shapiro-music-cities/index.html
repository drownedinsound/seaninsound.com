How Do We Save Our Music Scenes? Meet Sound Diplomacy’s Shain Shapiro

2 March 2025 · 40 min

Sean sits down with Shain Shapiro of Sound Diplomacy to ask what makes a great music city, and why music still is not treated as part of a city's economy. It gets into Live Nation, local media and where the next music hotspots might be.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Local music scenes need infrastructure, investment and policy, not just artists.
  2. Governments should treat music as an economic and public good, like transit or housing.
  3. Live Nation-style monopolies shape local music ecosystems.
  4. Local media and community spaces help scenes survive.
  5. Cities can future-proof music against climate, economic and tech shifts.

Who you will hear

Shain Shapiro

Founder, Sound Diplomacy. Shain Shapiro founded Sound Diplomacy and advises cities and governments on investing in music.
