Meet The AI Rapper Beloved By The Far-Right - Part 1

1 September 2026 · 47 min

Effie Webb and Harriet Williamson explain how they traced Danny Bones, an AI rapper with millions of views, to the Node Project and links with the far-right party Advance UK. They also ask whether reporting on him boosts his platform.

Key takeaways

  1. Danny Bones is AI-generated, created by the Node Project, and passed five million YouTube views in ten months.
  2. The reporting tied the Node Project to Advance UK and found a music producer and a video producer behind it.
  3. Harriet thinks the aesthetic looks like someone watched This Is England and missed everything else about it.
  4. The episode weighs the economics of streams against political commissions.
