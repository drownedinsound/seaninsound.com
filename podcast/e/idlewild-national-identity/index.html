Is national identity in music a good thing? In conversation with Idlewild

18 September 2025 · 54 min

Roddy Woomble and Rod Jones of Idlewild talk to Sean about what makes a national sound and what Scotland means to the band. They cover Patti Smith, Sonic Youth and their self-titled album after a six-year gap.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Idlewild felt like outsiders in London and closer to American bands than Britpop.
  2. Patti Smith and Sonic Youth's Daydream Nation shaped their approach.
  3. Scottish music communities support each other regionally.
  4. Rod Jones produced the new album, capturing melodic chaos.
  5. Rock refuses to die.
