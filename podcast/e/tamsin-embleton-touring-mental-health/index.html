DiS008: Live Music And Mental Health

20 April 2023 · 46 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Therapist Tamsin Embleton talks to Sean about her book Touring and Mental Health: The Music Industry Manual. It looks at the realities of life on the road and what simple changes the industry could make.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. The book has contributions from mental health experts on everything from breath-work to nutrition.
  2. It includes interviews with artists such as Radiohead, Nile Rodgers, Four Tet and Chvrches' Lauren Mayberry.
  3. Touring with Nick Cave and Anna Calvi fed into the early stages of the book.
  4. Live Nation bought 3000 copies to put in dressing rooms.
  5. The conversation covers dressing room environments and dealing with the media.

Who you will hear

Tamsin Embleton

Therapist, founder, editor of the manual, Music Industry Therapist Collective. Therapist and live music expert, author of Touring and Mental Health: The Music Industry Manual.
