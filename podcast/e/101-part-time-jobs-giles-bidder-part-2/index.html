From 500 Podcasts to Radio 1: DiS meets 101 Part Time Jobs (Part 2)

20 January 2026 · 51 min

Part two turns the lens on the medium itself, with Giles Bidder on interviewing, editing ethics and building trust. It goes from Shaun Keaveny's Community Garden Radio to bad bosses and Radio 1.

Key takeaways

  1. Good interviews need space to breathe, and editing carries ethical weight.
  2. It is about care: how to hold people well and listen properly.
  3. Giles talks about producing Community Garden Radio as a lesson in warmth.
  4. He talks about bad bosses, fear-based workplaces and the quiet anger behind the show.
