Should music critics ’pivot to video’?

4 February 2024 · 52 min · with Sean Adams and Helena Wadia

Jimmy Watkins, once an 800m finalist and guitarist in Future of the Left, reviews records while running in the Welsh countryside. Sean asks where running reviews came from and whether critics should pivot to video.

About this show

Conversations about music, power and what comes next, with the artists and campaigners trying to fix the system. Music is upstream from politics. Hosted by Sean Adams and Helena Wadia.

About the show · New here? Start here · About and the hosts

Key takeaways

  1. Jimmy reached the 800m final at the 2006 World Indoor Championships before playing guitar in Future of the Left.
  2. Running Punks became a community of music-loving runners.
  3. The episode opens with a running review of The Art of Losing by The Anchoress.
  4. His music writing bug started with music biographies and magazines in Tesco.
  5. He is working on a documentary about running across America and a project called Joyce.

Who you will hear

Jimmy Watkins

YouTube music critic, musician, Running Punks. Former athlete and Future of the Left guitarist who reviews records while running.
