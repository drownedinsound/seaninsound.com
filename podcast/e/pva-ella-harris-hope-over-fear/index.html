Why Hope Over Fear Trumps No Music On A Dead Planet: DiS meets PVA’s Ella Harris

3 February 2026 · 62 min

Ella Harris, front-person of PVA and Campaigns Manager at Music Declares Emergency, explains the shift from No Music On A Dead Planet to the Hope Over Fear campaign. It builds action hubs in grassroots venues where fans and locals organise around the climate crisis.

Key takeaways

  1. After five years of No Music On A Dead Planet, Hope Over Fear moves from awareness to action.
  2. Action hubs in grassroots venues give fans and communities a physical place to organise.
  3. Touring economics get in the way: trains cost £150 while flights are £30.
  4. Ella argues music fandom is an empathetic practice that translates to organising power.
